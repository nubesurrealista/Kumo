This will be the last release of Kumo, the project will be archived.

This is a personal decision, I just DON’T LIKE the development of Mihon, they make developing a fork increasingly hard.

So instead of adapting to a development that I do not like, I decided to simply abandon this.

If you want an alternative, I recommend [Usagi](https://github.com/UsagiApp/Usagi), for me it's what I tried to do with Kumo, but with nothing to do with Mihon. 

This release is based on unreleased Mihon code, with changes like the new decoder that it's horrible for low-end devices, Usagi on the other hand is much more efficient even on low-end.

Well, see you in my next project I guess, thanks to those who used Kumo, and if you see repos or modified versions of Kumo, know that they are probably malicious.
